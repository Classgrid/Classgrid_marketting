## 🎯 **KEY DIFFERENCES BY INSTITUTION TYPE**

### **🏫 Schools (K-12 Focus)**
- **Core Focus**: Basic academic management, attendance, homework
- **Limited Advanced Features**: No viva, library, alumni for younger students
- **Cafeteria**: Basic canteen management included
- **Certifications**: Basic digital certificates

### **🎯 Coaching Institutes (Exam Preparation)**
- **Core Focus**: Intensive exam preparation and assessment
- **Test Series**: Complete test series management system
- **Mock Exams**: AI-powered mock test generation
- **Rank Prediction**: CET/JEE rank prediction analytics
- **CET/NEET/JEE Practice Exams**: Authentic competitive exam creation
- **Practice Mode**: Unlimited practice attempts
- **No Facilities**: No canteen, library management
- **Quick Setup**: Streamlined for test prep centers

### **🏛️ Colleges (Higher Education)**
- **Full Features**: Complete academic and administrative suite
- **Advanced Assessments**: Viva, library, alumni management
- **Compliance**: Audit trails for accreditation
- **Facilities**: Full canteen and library management

### **🏗️ Engineering Colleges (Technical Excellence)**
- **All College Features** + Engineering Enhancements
- **Technical Labs**: Lab management and equipment tracking
- **Industry Connect**: Partnerships, internships, placements
- **Research Focus**: Publication and patent tracking
- **Advanced Compliance**: NBA/NAAC specific requirements
- **Innovation Hub**: Project management and technical societies
