## Body (English)

Big milestone for Classgrid today! 🚀

When we set out to build the next-generation operating system for education, we knew that reliability would be our most important feature. Schools, colleges, and coaching centers cannot afford downtime, and they certainly cannot afford missed communications. Today, we took a massive step forward in securing that reliability.

We are incredibly proud to announce two major infrastructural milestones that set Classgrid up for serious scale.

### 1. Amazon SES Production Access (50,000 Emails/Day) 📧

We have officially received our Production Access approval for Amazon Simple Email Service (SES). 

For an EdTech platform, email deliverability is a matter of trust. Whether it is an OTP for secure login, an urgent fee reminder, or a real-time exam notification, these messages must hit the inbox instantly—never the spam folder. With this approval, AWS has verified our domain reputation and granted us the capacity to send up to **50,000 critical transactional emails per day** with flawless deliverability.

### 2. Backed by AWS Activate Founders ☁️

But it gets even better! We have officially been accepted into the prestigious **AWS Activate Founders** program. 

Amazon has granted Classgrid **$1,000 (approximately ₹96,000)** in cloud computing credits. For a bootstrapped startup, having AWS Startups back our vision gives us an incredible runway. This grant allows us to confidently deploy our heavy-duty infrastructure without worrying about immediate server costs. 

**How we are using the AWS infrastructure:**
* **Amazon EC2:** Highly scalable, dedicated compute power to host the core Classgrid operating system securely and without lag.
* **Amazon SES:** Ensuring 100% deliverability for our daily transactional emails, OTPs, and critical notifications.
* **Amazon SNS:** Powering our real-time SMS and push notification infrastructure, guaranteeing that parents and students never miss an urgent update.
* **Amazon Bedrock:** The AWS Activate credits allow us to immediately integrate cutting-edge generative AI features via Amazon Bedrock, automating heavy administrative tasks for teachers and institutions.
