### 3. Accepted into MongoDB for Startups 🍃

And there is more. Classgrid has been accepted into the **MongoDB for Startups** program — one of the most sought-after startup programs in the developer ecosystem.

MongoDB is the database engine that powers Classgrid — every student record, every attendance log, every fee transaction, every classroom interaction runs on MongoDB Atlas. Having MongoDB officially back our platform is a massive vote of confidence.

**What MongoDB for Startups gives us:**
* **Up to $5,000 in MongoDB Atlas credits (approximately ₹4,20,000)** — covering our managed database hosting for the foreseeable future.
* **1:1 technical advisor sessions** — direct architectural guidance from MongoDB engineers to help us optimize our data layer as we scale.
* **MongoDB University access** — 150+ labs and courses to sharpen our database engineering.
* **24/7 Developer Support** — priority support for production issues.
* **Co-marketing opportunities** — exposure through MongoDB's global developer ecosystem and startup network.
* **Partner ecosystem access** — matched credits from MongoDB's integration partners.
