## Body (English)

Every piece of data that flows through Classgrid — student records, attendance, fees, exams, chat, notifications — lives in MongoDB Atlas. It is the single most important piece of technology in our stack.

We applied to the **MongoDB for Startups** program in early July 2026, hoping to get official support from the company behind our database. We followed up three to four times. No reply. After a month of silence, we assumed it was a no.

Then tonight, this landed in our inbox:

**Official Email from the MongoDB for Startups Team:**

> Welcome to the MongoDB for Startups
>
> program
>
> Hi Nikhil,
>

We are in.

### What MongoDB for Startups Gives Us

The program is built around a tiered system (Inspire, Grow, Innovate, Scale) that supports startups from prototype to production. Companies already in the program represent over **$200 billion in combined valuation**. Here is what we now have access to:

#### 🍃 MongoDB Atlas Credits
Credits to cover our cloud database hosting on Atlas, reducing our infrastructure spend as we scale to more institutions.

#### 🧑‍💻 1:1 Technical Advisors
Direct sessions with MongoDB engineers for guidance on architecture, query optimization, and scaling our data layer across multiple institution types.

#### 🎓 MongoDB University
Access to **150+ hands-on labs** covering aggregation pipelines, Atlas Search, indexing strategies, and more.

#### 🛟 24/7 Developer Support
Priority support with faster response times — essential when your database powers an entire school's daily operations.

#### 🤖 Voyage AI & Partner Credits
The program now includes tokens for **Voyage AI** (advanced embedding and reranking models), plus matched credits from technology partners like **Fireworks AI** and **Temporal**.

#### 🤝 Go-to-Market Opportunities
Co-marketing and visibility through MongoDB's global network of startups and developers.

### What This Means

MongoDB is the third major infrastructure company — after AWS and Cloudflare — to officially back Classgrid. The company that stores every single byte of our data now stands behind what we are building.

We did not expect this acceptance. But it arrived exactly when we needed it most.

Thank you, MongoDB. We will put every credit and every resource to work.
