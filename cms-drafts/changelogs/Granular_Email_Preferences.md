# Introducing Granular Email Preferences

We understand that everyone uses Classgrid differently, and you should have full control over the emails you receive from us. Previously, subscribing to our updates was an all-or-nothing choice. Today, we are excited to introduce Granular Email Preferences.

You now have the power to independently opt-in or opt-out of three distinct notification categories:

- **Blog Posts**: Educational articles, campus administration insights, and best practices from our team.
- **Product Changelogs**: Be the first to know about new features, bug fixes, and platform improvements.
- **Legal Notices**: Important updates to our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and other compliance documents.

This update ensures that you only receive the information that matters most to you, keeping your inbox clean and relevant. 

**How to manage your settings:**
You won't find these settings cluttering up your Classgrid platform dashboard. Instead, you can easily customize your preferences at any time by clicking the **Manage Preferences / Unsubscribe** link at the very bottom of any email you receive from us.
